Emphasis and strengthened text

Before you submit your assignment, you really should double-check your work. Late submissions will not be accepted under any circumstances. If you have questions, it is always better to ask early rather than the night before the deadline.

... Separating content from presentation

Explanation

<em> changes the tone of a sentence. Moving it changes the meaning:

I didn't eat the cake. (Someone else did.)

I didn't eat the cake. (I ate something else.)

<strong> shows that something is important:

Warning: the stove is hot.

<i> and <b> only make text italic or bold. They don't say why. Screen readers can use <em> and <strong> to change their voice, so people who can't see the page still hear the emphasis and importance. That's why <em> and <strong> are the better choice.
